U.S. Geological Survey (USGS), such as elevation data of the 3D Elevation

    "When using information from USGS information products, publications, or
    websites, we ask that proper credit be given. Credit can be provided by
    including a citation such as the following: Credit: U.S. Geological
    Survey"

Source: https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits
(read 2026-11-15).
