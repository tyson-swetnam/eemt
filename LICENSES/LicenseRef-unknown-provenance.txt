LicenseRef-unknown-provenance

Data of unrecorded origin. Not covered by the licence of this repository.

Files carrying this identifier are sample data whose source, author and terms
of use are not recorded anywhere in the repository or in the files themselves.
Because the origin is unknown, no licence can be stated for them: they are NOT
asserted to be under the BSD-3-Clause licence of this repository, and they are
not asserted to be in the public domain.

They are kept because the example commands and the tests use them. Do not
redistribute them outside this repository on the assumption that they are
freely licensed.

When the repository owner states the source of a file, its entry in REUSE.toml
is replaced by the real copyright holder and licence, and this identifier is
dropped for that file.
